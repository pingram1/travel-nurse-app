# Travel Nurse App — User Playbook

A practical guide for travel nurses, physicians, and the people who support them on assignment.

**Developed by Start Right Tutoring, LLC**

---

## Welcome

Travel Nurse App is your assignment command center. Instead of juggling airline tabs, housing listings, ride apps, and a folder of license photos, you plan the trip in one place—then keep using it while you are on contract.

You can:

- Search a hospital or plan around a city
- Find lodging that fits your **housing stipend** and safety preferences
- Search flights, book on the airline, and import your boarding pass
- Save dining, groceries, gyms, and entertainment near your stay
- Choose Uber/Lyft **or** a weekly rental (not both as the primary mode)
- See a safety grade for the facility
- Track take-home pay against lodging cost
- Store BLS, ACLS, license, and TB documents in a secure vault

Bookings for flights, hotels, Airbnb, and rides finish in those companies’ apps or sites. This app keeps the itinerary, the safety context, and your documents together.

## Getting Started

### 1. Onboarding

Open the app and complete the short welcome (or tap **New here? Complete onboarding** from Sign In). You will see three things the app is built around:

1. **Credential Vault** — licenses and certs in on-device secure storage  
2. **Stipend Tracker** — housing stipend and tax home  
3. **Safety routing** — OSHA- and crime-aware filters for assignments  

Then tap **Continue to Sign In**.

Accounts use a password of 12 or more characters with uppercase, lowercase, and a number.

### 2. Sign in

Enter your work email and password, then tap **Sign In**. You land on **Your Trip Hub**.

If you already have an account, the app restores your session on launch so you do not sign in every time.

### 3. Set up your profile (recommended first)

Open **Profile** (bottom tab, or tap your avatar on Trip Hub).

1. Tap the photo to add an avatar from your library.  
2. Enter first name, last name, email, and optional phone. Tap **Save profile**.  
3. Under **Payment**, save card brand, last four digits, and expiry (never a full card number). Tap **Save payment method**.  
4. If you want Pro safety detail, choose Monthly, Semi-annual, or Annual (you need a payment method first).

Your name and photo appear on Trip Hub after you save.

### 4. Set tax home and stipend (recommended before lodging)

Open the **Stipend** tab:

1. Enter **gross weekly pay** and **daily housing stipend**.  
2. Enter tax-home street, city, 2-letter state, and ZIP.  
3. Tap **Save contract details**.  
4. Optionally tap **Upload contract photo** to keep a picture of the contract with your records.

Tax home city and state can autofill your **departure airport** on Trip Hub. Daily stipend filters lodging so over-budget stays are flagged.

## Core Workflows

The bottom tabs are **Trip**, **Safety**, **Stipend**, **Vault**, and **Profile**.

Trip Hub has two modes:

- **Planning** — build the itinerary  
- **Travel** — after you confirm; logs and City Finder while you are there  

### Creating an itinerary

Complete these in order (Housing-First is on by default). Facility and flights are optional; lodging, one dining pick, and ground transport are required to confirm.

#### Step 1 — Facility or city

On **Trip Hub**:

- **Hospital assignment:** Type the facility name in the search field and select it from the list.  
- **No facility yet:** Tap **Skip facility — plan by city**, enter a city (for example `Houston, TX`), and tap **Set destination**.

Lodging, flights, City Finder, and ground transport all use this destination.

#### Step 2 — Contract dates

Tap the start date, then the end date, and save. Flight search stays locked until dates are saved.

#### Step 3 — Housing-First (optional toggle)

Leave **Housing-First mode** on to choose lodging before flights. Turn it off if you need to lock a flight first.

#### Step 4 — Lodging

The app lists hotels and short-term stays within **25 miles**, safest first, and prefers nightly rates at or under your daily stipend.

For each listing you will see:

- Nightly rate and distance  
- Stipend variance (under / at / over)  
- Area label: **Preferred**, **Standard**, or **Review area**

Tap a listing to select it. Then tap **Book on hotel site** or **Book on Airbnb** to finish booking with that partner. Come back to the app—your selection stays on the itinerary.

**Pro:** Open a selected card to see crime-index reasoning (“why this rating”). Basic users see the labels only; upgrade on Profile if you want the detail.

#### Step 5 — Flights (optional)

1. Confirm the 3-letter **departure airport** (suggested from tax home when set).  
2. Tap **Update flight search**.  
3. Tap a flight to select it, then book in the **airline app or website**.  
4. When you have a ticket, import the boarding pass (next section).  

You can tap **Skip flights for now** and add a flight later.

#### Step 6 — City Finder

Open **City Finder** from the Trip Hub shortcuts or the workflow bar.

- In planning, use the tabs: **All**, **Dining**, **Groceries**, **Gym**, **Entertainment**.  
- Tap a place to save it. You need **at least one dining pick** to confirm the itinerary. Groceries, gyms, and entertainment are optional.  
- Places are within **50 miles** of your destination or selected lodging.

#### Step 7 — Ground transportation

Choose **one** primary mode:

- **Ride-share** — Uber or Lyft for airport pickup. The app suggests a pickup time about 45 minutes after arrival (bags). Open the provider app from the card.  
- **Car rental** — Turo vehicle types, Enterprise, or Hertz near the arrival airport. Open the partner app or site to book.

Switching modes clears the other selection so the itinerary stays simple.

#### Step 8 — Review and confirm

Open **Itinerary Summary** (or **Summary** on Trip Hub). You will see facility, dates, lodging, flight and seat, dining, entertainment, ground transport, estimated total, and percent complete.

When lodging, dining, and ground are done, tap **Confirm itinerary**. Trip Hub switches to **Travel mode**.

To change plans later, tap **Edit itinerary**.

### Importing a boarding pass and using Seat Locator

Use this after you book on the airline—or anytime you need to attach a ticket.

1. On Trip Hub, tap **Pass**, **Scan boarding pass into itinerary**, or **Import boarding pass / update flight later**.  
2. **Upload from photo library** or **Take photo**.  
3. If the photo is blurry, paste the pass text (airline, flight number, airports, seat, date) and tap **Parse text**.  
4. Check extracted fields and confidence, then tap **Add to itinerary**.  

If a seat was found, **Seat Locator** opens. A marker shows where that seat sits on a generic aircraft map. You can type a seat such as `14C` if you need to correct it.

Seat Locator does **not** sell seats. Booking always happens with the airline.

### Travel mode (while you are on assignment)

After confirm, Trip Hub shows a **Traveling** badge, your destination, and the itinerary dashboard.

**If you selected a rental**

Use the **Rental log**:

- Start and end mileage  
- Insurance on file, plus an insurance photo  
- Photos: before, after, fuel (add a fuel note if you want)  
- Remove a photo if you attached the wrong one  

**If you selected Uber or Lyft**

Use the **Ride-share log**: add a screenshot from your library or take a photo of the receipt.

**While you are there**

- **City Finder** focuses on saved dining plus nearby gyms and entertainment.  
- **Summary** reopens the full itinerary.

### Safety Intelligence

Open the **Safety** tab after you select a facility.

- You will see the facility name, address, and an overall grade (**A–F**).  
- Grades combine BLS OSHA compliance, FBI NIBRS residential crime data, and verified reviews from nurses and physicians.  
- Lodging on Trip Hub uses the same data: Preferred / Standard / Review.

**Basic:** overall grade.  
**Pro:** OSHA score, crime index (lower is safer), nurse-verified rating and review count, plus crime-index reasoning on lodging cards.

If you skipped the facility and planned by city only, pick a hospital on Trip Hub to unlock facility grades.

---

### Stipend Tracker

On the **Stipend** tab:

1. Save pay, daily stipend, and tax home (see Getting Started).  
2. Review the snapshot: weekly pay, daily stipend, tax home, **estimated take-home**.  
3. Optionally turn on **Highlight tax deductibility** for a reminder that the subscription may be a professional expense—confirm with a tax advisor.  
4. **Lodging vs. stipend variance** lists stays at or under your daily rate once lodging is loaded from Trip Hub.

Change pay or stipend anytime and tap **Save contract details** again. Trip Hub lodging filters update from the daily rate.

---

### Credential Vault

Open the **Vault** tab. Four slots:

| Slot | Typical document |
|---|---|
| State RN / license | License card or photo |
| BLS | AHA or equivalent card |
| ACLS | ACLS card |
| TB test / IGRA | Most recent negative result |

**Upload or replace**

1. Tap **Upload** (or **Replace file**).  
2. Choose an image from your library.  
3. Status shows **VALID**, **EXPIRING** (within 30 days), or **EXPIRED**.

Your role controls what you can do:

- **Nurses and physicians** can upload and replace.  
- **HR** can view and **export** credential records for agency or facility files.  
- **Administrators** can upload, replace, delete, and export.

If you do not have write access, the vault explains that an administrator must grant it.

Credentials stay on the device in secure storage. They are not stored with your trip itinerary.

## Tips & Best Practices

### Get more from Pro

Pro is worth it when you are comparing neighborhoods, not just nightly rates.

- On **Safety**, use the score breakdown (OSHA, crime index, nurse reviews) before you commit to a city.  
- On lodging cards, read **Pro · why this rating** so “Review area” is a reason, not a surprise.  
- Upgrade, change plan, or cancel anytime on **Profile**.

### City Finder

- Save dining early; it is required to confirm.  
- Use **Groceries** and **Gym** in the first 48 hours on assignment so you are not hunting after a night shift.  
- Entertainment is optional and shows on the summary when you save it.  
- In Travel mode, start from saved dining, then expand to gyms and entertainment nearby.

### Housing stipend

- Set tax home and daily stipend **before** you browse lodging.  
- Prefer **Preferred area** listings that also sit under stipend.  
- Housing-First keeps you from buying a flight before you know housing will work.

### Flights and passes

- Flights are optional to confirm. If the contract is driving, skip flights and confirm lodging + dining + ground.  
- Import the boarding pass as soon as the airline issues it so seat and flight sit on the itinerary.  
- Keep a screenshot of the pass in your camera roll; re-import if the airline changes your seat.

### Ground transport

- Ride-share is usually enough for a short stay near the hospital.  
- Choose a weekly rental if you will commute nights or the lodging is not transit-friendly.  
- In Travel mode, log mileage and photos at pickup and return—agencies and rental desks ask for this when something goes wrong.

### Credentials

- Upload all four slots before you travel. **Expiring** gives you a 30-day heads-up.  
- HR and admin users: export from Vault when a facility requests a credential packet, rather than texting photos.

|---|---|

The app supports **nurse**, **physician**, **HR**, and **administrator** accounts. Vault buttons (upload, delete, export) follow your role. If something you need is missing, ask your agency administrator to update access.

At the bottom of **Profile**, tap **Sign out**. This clears secure session credentials on the device.

- Trip Hub empty states tell you the next required step (dates, airport, facility, or city).  
- If hospital search, lodging, or City Finder cannot load, check your connection and try again; the app uses live maps and facility data.  
- For account, billing, or vault access issues, contact your agency administrator or Start Right Tutoring, LLC support channel used by your organization.
